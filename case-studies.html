Challenge

Fill seats across multiple institutions (BCom, BBA, CAFM streams) with qualified admission enquiries — season after season, at a predictable cost.

Strategy

  • Always-on Meta lead campaigns per program & intake date
  • Google Search for high-intent admission queries
  • Landing pages + Google Business Profile + local SEO
  • Weekly CPL optimization and creative testing

Result

Across the account, campaigns delivered 2,490+ leads with CPL ranging ₹82–₹140 and CTRs up to 2.94%. The best single campaign (Smart BCom) generated 465 leads at ₹102. See the live breakdown on the Performance page.

Challenge

Scale a women-owned D2C oil brand profitably — drive repeat purchases of hero SKUs like the ₹349 groundnut oil while keeping acquisition efficient.

Strategy

  • Seasonal Meta sales campaigns (Diwali, Raksha Bandhan, month-end)

Challenge

Turn a nutritionist's personal brand into a consistent consultation-booking machine — across "best nutritionist", PCOS and gut-health searches.

Strategy

  • High-intent Google Search + call & appointment extensions
  • Topic-cluster SEO content for PCOS / gut health / weight
  • GEO so the brand surfaces inside AI answers
  • Book-appointment & WhatsApp conversion actions

Result

Search campaigns drove 196 phone calls, 117 booked appointments and 2,367 outbound clicks; the "Best Nutritionist" campaign alone reached 109K impressions & 12K clicks. In parallel, SEO grew the site to 253K impressions / ~2K clicks at avg position 6.1. Full graphs on the SEO page.

Challenge

Grow a creator's YouTube channel and promote podcast episodes with founders and experts — cheaply and at scale.

Strategy

  • YouTube subscriber campaign at rock-bottom cost/sub
  • Per-episode video view campaigns for each guest
  • Thumbnail + hook testing to lift interaction rate

Result

The subscriber campaign delivered 17,853 subscribers at ₹3.02 each on 1.6M impressions and 113K engagements; episode campaigns hit interaction rates up to 69%. Watch cuts on the Video page.
